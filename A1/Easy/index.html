POKEMON GEN I
PIKACHU
Pikachu is one of the most iconic Pokémon, known for its bright yellow fur and red cheeks. ⚡ It’s an Electric-type Pokémon that stores energy in its body and releases powerful lightning attacks. Pikachu is friendly, loyal, and often seen alongside its trainer, Ash, in the Pokémon series. Its cute appearance and strong abilities have made it a global symbol of the Pokémon franchise.
